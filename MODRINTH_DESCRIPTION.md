# OpenStage 🎬✨
> **Professional In-Game Animation Studio & Machinima Suite for Minecraft 1.21.1 (Fabric)**

**OpenStage** turns Minecraft into a powerful, all-in-one animation and cinematic suite. Animate player characters with skeletal bending, manage camera paths, synchronize audio tracks, craft animated 3D title plates, and polish motion with Bézier curves and onion skinning — **all directly inside the game without ever leaving Minecraft!**

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 🕺 Skeletal Bending & Rigging
* **Built-in Player Bends:** Animate Steve and Alex with natural elbow and knee bending out of the box — no external mods required!
* **Interactive 3D Gizmos:** Rotate and translate limbs directly in the 3D viewport using color-coded rotation rings and movement handles.
* **Pose Presets:** Instant access to pre-made poses: *T-Pose, Idle, Walk, Run, Wave, Sitting, Sneak, Victory, Clap, Fight, Dance*, and more.
* **Pose Copy & Paste:** Copy a pose from one actor or keyframe and paste it onto any other character or timestamp in seconds.

### ⏱️ Pro Multi-Track Timeline
* **Full Multi-Track Editing:** Independent tracks for **Actor Bones**, **Cinematic Camera**, **Particles**, **3D Text Plates**, and **Audio**.
* **Dope Sheet & Keyframe Management:** Add (`+◆`), delete (`🗑`), clear (`✂`), box-select, and drag keyframes freely across the timeline.
* **Graph Editor & Bézier Curves:** Fine-tune easing and motion acceleration with interactive Bézier tangent handles or choose from industry-standard presets (*Ease In, Ease Out, Bounce, Elastic, Exponential*).
* **Onion Skinning (Калька):** View ghosted silhouettes of previous keyframes to ensure perfectly smooth character transitions.

### 🎵 In-Game Audio Synchronization
* **Waveform Timeline:** Import your music and voice acting files directly into the project.
* **Cut & Trim (`♫ Разрез`):** Split audio segments on the fly, adjust start offsets, and sync character actions to the beat with frame-perfect precision.

### 📝 Animated 3D Text Cards (Subtitles & Titles)
* **On-Screen & In-World Titles:** Create subtitles, cinematic chapter titles, and credits directly on the timeline.
* **Visual Effects:** Built-in transition effects including *Fade, Slide Up, Typewriter, 3D Flip, Neon Pulse*, and *Glitch Decode*.

### 📦 Custom Models & Instant Skins
* **Model Palette:** Import and preview `.bbmodel` (Blockbench), `.obj`, and Bedrock `.geo.json` models.
* **Skin Manager:** Switch actor skins instantly from your `openstage/skins` folder without restarting the game.

### 🎥 Cinematic Camera & World Scan
* **Camera Pathing:** Place camera keyframes with smooth positional and rotational interpolation.
* **Real-time Camera Mode:** Preview cinematic camera shots directly in the live world.
* **World Scan Mode:** Toggle between clean Chroma Green/Blue studio backgrounds or live in-world scanning of your Minecraft terrain and sky.

## ⌨️ Controls & Keybindings

| Action | Control |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Open Editor** | `O` *(or `/openstage` command)* |
| **Orbit Camera** | `Right Click + Drag` in viewport |
| **Pan Camera** | `Left Click + Drag` on empty viewport |
| **Zoom Viewport** | `Mouse Wheel Scroll` |
| **Transform Bone** | `Left Click + Drag` on red/green/blue Gizmo rings |
| **Play / Pause** | `Spacebar` |
| **Scrub Timeline** | `Left Click + Drag` on timeline ruler |
| **Box Select Keys**| `Shift + Left Click + Drag` on timeline |
| **Undo / Redo** | `Ctrl + Z` / `Ctrl + Y` |
| **Exit Camera Mode**| `ESC` |

## 🛠️ Requirements & Installation

1. Install **Fabric Loader** (version `0.19.5` or newer) for **Minecraft 1.21.1**.
2. Install **Fabric API**.
3. Place `openstage-1.0-release.jar` into your `.minecraft/mods` directory.
4. *(Optional & Recommended)* Fully compatible with **Sodium** and **Iris Shaders** for optimal performance and cinematic visuals.
